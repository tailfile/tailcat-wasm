How measurements work

Test speeds use received payload bytes over the last 2 seconds. Transport speeds above count encrypted packets, including overhead and retransmissions. Transport upload counts local sends, not confirmed delivery. The final average includes streaming, payload verification, and the receiver’s completion receipt.
